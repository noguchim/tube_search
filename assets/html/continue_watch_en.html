Selecting videos

  • Only videos with a green check are played in sequence.
  • Clear a check to skip that video.
  • Live streams and similar videos may not be available for selection.

History and playlists

  • Select an earlier playlist in History to continue or watch it again.
  • Use the History menu to rename or delete a playlist.
